# Code of Conduct

Market Microcosm Lab welcomes rigorous disagreement and careful criticism.

Participants should:

- critique ideas, models, assumptions, and evidence rather than people;
- state uncertainty and conflicts of interest when relevant;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted abuse;
- keep research discussions reproducible and evidence-oriented;
- correct material errors openly when discovered.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that repeatedly undermines a safe, good-faith research environment.

For research disputes, prefer a falsifiable experiment over an argument by authority.
