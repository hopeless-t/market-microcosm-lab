# Research protocol

## Two coupled loops

### L1 — ecosystem policy improvement
1. Freeze world, observer, verifier, Root of Trust, and scenario split.
2. Generate policy challengers on discovery scenarios.
3. Evaluate challengers in shadow simulation.
4. Re-evaluate incumbent and selected challenger on untouched promotion scenarios.
5. Verifier applies hard constraints before performance comparisons.
6. Promote or reject; persist evidence and manifest.

### L2 — improvement-system improvement
1. Treat observation design, search width, simulation budget, horizon, estimator and stress generation as candidate machinery.
2. Run complete L1 instances under each machinery candidate.
3. Compare machinery on held-out meta evidence: survival, oracle gap, generalization and search cost.
4. Promote the machinery configuration, not just the resulting policy.
5. A Root-of-Trust change creates a new constitutional generation and invalidates inherited certification.

## Three truth levels

- World truth: full latent simulator state.
- Operational truth: what the Governor can observe.
- Certification truth: independently persisted evidence accepted by the verifier.

Never collapse these into one object.

## Completion criterion for a research increment

An increment is not complete merely because a better score appears. It is complete when:
- result is replayable;
- invariants pass;
- discovery and holdout evidence are separate;
- exact small-world checks still pass;
- assumptions and model version are documented;
- failure cases are retained;
- the result says what evidence would falsify it.
