# ODD addendum — E097 real transient-stress negative control

## Purpose

E035–E037 build structural early warnings from Japanese negative evidence.

E097 adds a real **recovery control** so the warning system does not overfit every temporary KPI deterioration into structural failure.

## GVA TECH FY2025 LegalTech SaaS

Published KPI sequence:

- Q2: ARR 796m JPY, net revenue churn 0.50%;
- Q3: ARR 764m JPY, churn 0.95%;
- Q4: ARR 830m JPY, churn 0.83%.

Q2 -> Q3 therefore shows:

`ARR -32m AND churn +0.45pp`

A one-quarter sign rule would label structural failure.

But Q3 -> Q4 shows:

`ARR +66m AND churn -0.12pp`

The company states the Q3 deterioration came from cancellations among non-main-target customers and that the temporary effect converged, with ARR and churn recovering.

## Consequence

`ARR down + churn up`

is a stress signal, not a sufficient structural-failure certificate.

Persistence, mechanism evidence, or prospective holdout survival is required before escalation to structural-failure authority.

Promotion:

`one-quarter-arr-down-churn-up-does-not-certify-structural-saas-failure-v1`
