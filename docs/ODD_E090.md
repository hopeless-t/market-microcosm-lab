# ODD addendum — E090 post-exit revenue / profitability sign reversal

## Purpose

E029 separates strategic exit from insolvency-style failure.

E090 returns to the Allied Architects lineage after the SuperFaction exit and checks the next realized operating period.

## FY2025 Q1 public result

Compared with FY2024 Q1:

- consolidated revenue: **827 -> 809 million JPY** (`-18`, reported `-2.2%`);
- operating profit/loss: **-229 -> +16 million JPY** (`+245` swing).

The company states that the main reason for the revenue decline was the loss of SuperFaction revenue contribution after the exit.

It separately states that overseas-business restructuring and cost reduction produced positive operating profit.

## Scalar conflict

A revenue-only evaluator says:

`WORSE`

An operating-profit evaluator says:

`BETTER`

Both are numerically correct about their own dimension.

Therefore neither dimension is an adequate scalar health label for post-exit viability.

## Consequence

A deliberate portfolio contraction can remove revenue while improving operating economics.

This is a realized public-data complement to E029's structural strategic-exit model.

Promotion:

`strategic-exit-can-lower-revenue-while-improving-operating-profit-v1`

## Limitation

One company-quarter does not identify a universal strategic-exit effect size. Management explanations are retained as annotations rather than promoted to a general causal law.
