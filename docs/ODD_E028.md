# ODD addendum — E028 Japanese negative-evidence corpus

## Purpose

Counter survivorship bias by admitting Japanese withdrawal, sunset, pivot, portfolio-pruning, and bankruptcy evidence into a dedicated negative-evidence corpus.

Success-only calibration can make a market model look stable because the systems that disappeared are missing from the evidence plane.

E028 therefore asks which failure mechanisms are invisible in the current price/cost/churn pressure ladder.

## Initial evidence corpus

### BBD Initiative — portfolio pruning

Official FY2024 Q3 materials report rising churn while the group deliberately exits unprofitable services and migrates low-price customers upward.

From FY2023 Q4 to FY2024 Q3:

- ARR: 1,593 → 1,607 million JPY;
- churn: 1.15% → 2.33%;
- ARPA: 437,545 → 466,303 JPY;
- contracts: 3,641 → 3,416.

This is a pruning case, not a simple collapse trace.

### BBD Initiative — restructuring

Official FY2026 Q1 materials disclose:

- 730 million JPY of prior impairment;
- partial withdrawal from inside-sales support because customer-data accumulation was difficult and AI-as-a-Service fit was low;
- preparation to end or transfer Business Boost;
- an office fixed-cost reduction target of about 30%.

The failure mechanism includes strategic/data fit and resource concentration, not only demand.

### RickCloud — planned sunset

Ricksoft plans to stop new sales at the end of 2026, revise affected pricing from 2027, and end RickCloud in March 2029.

The stated reason is structural: Atlassian Cloud became the standard and Ricksoft judged it difficult to sustain the secure proprietary infrastructure and skilled engineering organization at the current price over the long term.

This exposes platform substitution plus maintenance/engineering burden.

### Leaner — product withdrawal after a no-sales year

Leaner describes a prior product that went roughly one year without selling. Even after revenue later appeared, management concluded the customer would not succeed and the company would not scale, and withdrew the product.

This exposes customer-success scalability as a failure axis distinct from immediate revenue.

### SalesNow — market-ceiling pivot

SalesNow's retrospective describes the pre-2022 business as having an estimated ARR ceiling of roughly 2–3 billion JPY despite deep customer pain. The company also describes monthly advertising spend in the tens of millions of JPY and five successive upsell products before the April 2022 complete withdrawal/pivot.

This exposes market ceiling, acquisition burn, and product sprawl.

### TDB — software bankruptcy context

TDB reports 195 software-industry bankruptcies through February FY2025, with 84.6% under 100 million JPY of debt. It also reports a 69.2% labor-shortage share in information services, rising wages, and longer cash-conversion periods in package software.

This is contextual evidence only. The category is broader than SaaS and must never be relabelled as a SaaS failure rate.

## Exact mechanism coverage

The declared initial negative-evidence mechanism set is:

- acquisition burn;
- cash-conversion lag;
- customer-success non-scalability;
- data-compounding misalignment;
- engineering-maintenance burden;
- labor-cost pressure;
- market ceiling;
- platform substitution;
- portfolio pruning;
- product sprawl.

E028 exhaustively enumerates evidence subsets and finds a minimum portfolio that covers every declared mechanism.

The purpose is not to claim these ten are exhaustive. The purpose is to make missing failure coverage machine-visible.

## Survivorship-bias guard

The current E011/E013 synthetic stress core focuses on price, platform operating cost, and baseline user churn.

E028 does not invalidate those axes. It shows they are insufficient as the entire empirical failure basis.

Future calibration must include both:

```text
survival evidence
+
negative evidence
```

A mechanism that explains only surviving systems cannot receive empirical adequacy authority.

## Promotion rule

`negative-evidence-corpus-required-for-market-calibration-v1`

## Limitation

The first corpus is small, heterogeneous, and intentionally conservative. Company postmortems are not independent audits, and aggregate software bankruptcy data are not a SaaS-only cohort.
