# ODD addendum — E085 real longitudinal holdout after KPI withdrawal

## Purpose

E079 keeps Allied Architects' deterioration-linked overseas-SaaS KPI withdrawal as an empirical event rather than dropping it as missing data.

E085 checks that decision against a later untouched public outcome.

## Timeline

**2024-02-14** — FY2023 results withdraw overseas-SaaS recurring KPIs after deterioration and many Q4 cancellations.

**2024-10-31** — 260 days later, the company resolves to dissolve the same overseas-business lineage and file winding-up proceedings for SUPERFACTION PTE. LTD. (renamed from CREADITS PTE. LTD. in July 2024); Creadits株式会社 is also dissolved.

The later notice states that the difficult business environment continued, early profitability improvement was judged extremely difficult, and continuation of the overseas subsidiary was judged difficult.

## Authority

E085 does **not** claim:

- the KPI withdrawal caused the later exit;
- every KPI withdrawal predicts liquidation;
- a liquidation probability or hazard ratio.

It supports a narrower claim:

> the withdrawal event is empirically relevant enough to remain in the longitudinal event record and should not be erased merely because its hidden KPI values are unavailable.
