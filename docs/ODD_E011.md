# ODD addendum — E011 pressure-knee experiment

E011 reuses the E010 entities and transition scheduling.

## New experimental factor

A scalar pressure level maps to three structural changes:

1. subscription price multiplier decreases;
2. platform operating-cost multiplier increases;
3. baseline churn increases.

The scan is deterministic conditional on mechanism, pressure level, and seed bank.

## Knee definition

The preliminary pressure knee for a mechanism is the first tested pressure level where observed full-horizon survival falls below 90%.

This is a localization device, not a universal constant. Follow-up work should refine the neighborhood with denser one-dimensional sweeps.

## Failure biopsy

A distinct seed bank is searched at the detected knee. The first failing trajectory is retained with monthly utility, diversity, actor counts, user population, platform cash, entries/exits, health, and conservation error.

This transfers the failure-state biopsy method from resource-pressure experiments into market ecology.
