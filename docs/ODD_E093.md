# ODD addendum — E093 Jooto traction / viability counterexample

## Purpose

E031 rejects short-run positive signals as sufficient PMF evidence.

E093 adds an independent 2026 Japanese SaaS exit with unusually explicit multi-axis public evidence.

## Public evidence

PR TIMES resolves on 2026-08-06 to end Jooto's general service on 2027-07-31 and abolish the Jooto business.

At the decision point the service is not traction-free:

- paid contracts (2026-07): **2,379 companies**;
- FY2026 revenue: **387m JPY**.

Yet the company states that despite pricing-plan revision, feature improvement, sales/CS strengthening, generative-AI features, and expansion of custom-development work, it did not establish sustainable competitive advantage in:

1. new customer acquisition;
2. existing-customer retention;
3. expansion within customer organizations;
4. enterprise expansion;
5. horizontal expansion of custom-development cases.

It also states:

- revenue remained below plan and turned negative YoY in the current year;
- operating losses had continued since the business began;
- FY2026 operating loss was **17m JPY**;
- latest Q1 operating loss was **16m JPY**.

The company explicitly weighs expected incremental growth/earnings against the opportunity cost of continued capital and human-resource investment and chooses reallocation.

## Counterexample

A naive rule:

`customers > 0 AND revenue > 0 => viable growth business`

returns a false positive.

## Consequence

Traction is not the same object as sustainable independent-growth viability.
