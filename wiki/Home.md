# Market Microcosm Lab

Market Microcosm Lab studies how a pooled-revenue market can remain alive as an ecosystem rather than maximize one actor's short-run return.

## Central question

What allocation and governance rules allow users, developers, publishers, and platforms to remain viable over long horizons while preserving quality, diversity, innovation, and entry?

## Research architecture

The laboratory contains:

- a World with authoritative simulated dynamics;
- an Oracle with complete information for exact checks only;
- an Observer defining deployable information;
- a Governor choosing interventions;
- an Experimenter running counterfactuals and stress tests;
- an independent Verifier;
- a Meta-Governor that improves the improvement machinery itself.

The optimizer cannot certify itself.
